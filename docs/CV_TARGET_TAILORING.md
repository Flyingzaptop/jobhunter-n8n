# Exact-vacancy CV selection and ATS evidence

`cv-factory-v2.3-exact-vacancy` generates a separate CV for the exact application. Its evidence plan reads actual analysis requirement quotations, the current source fragment catalog and explicitly named technology stack. Each detected ATS term is mapped to verified facts and source clauses, qualification caps, importance and supported/unsupported status. Mandatory terms weight 120, preferences 60, optional terms 20 and described stack terms 10. These exact terms outrank role-family defaults and change section/skill selection and order even between two vacancies in the same family. No fixed pair of CV variants is used.

The role family supplies background relevance where exact requirement evidence is absent. Title evidence takes priority over generic employer marketing. Engineering roles select actual engineering studies, embedded/control, CAD/prototyping and simulation evidence; IT support selects actual Windows/Active Directory/workstation work. Financial ML cannot qualify as FEA merely because the text contains “feature engineering”.

The writer receives exact requirements, a measurable keyword/evidence plan and a constrained grammar. Every supported detected ATS term must appear explicitly in a grounded claim, exact skill or deterministic education/language/training section. Missing supported coverage fails QA; unsupported keyword stuffing fails QA. A known missing strict mandatory qualification blocks generation before a model request. The manifest records `keyword_coverage` with the exact job/source/requirements/catalog hashes, terms, source facts and clauses, qualification levels, covered claim IDs and honest gaps. Requirements hashes include the job identity and source version, preventing reuse as a generic family plan.

Unrelated sections and skills are unavailable for selection. Target QA requires primary evidence in the headline, the first summary and at least half the summary words; the strongest target section must lead, and most of the first five skills must be relevant to the exact requirements or family. Academic, personal and independent research summaries require their honest provenance label. Relevant engineering education appears before projects; unrelated training is omitted. Basic-to-intermediate C++ and Linux familiarity remain capped. Existing fact/provenance and independent entailment checks remain active. Keyword detection uses verified candidate vocabulary, actual requirement skill labels and bounded technology aliases; matching a word never establishes an advanced qualification or years of work.
